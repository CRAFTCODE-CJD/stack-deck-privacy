# STACK//DECK: privacy

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

This policy covers STACK//DECK (Stack Deck) on Yandex Games, VK and Android. Features depend on the platform. Contact the developer about the game or your data at [channeljonathandeveloper@gmail.com](mailto:channeljonathandeveloper@gmail.com).

## Game data and saves

The game stores settings, deck contents, run progress and personal records on your device. You do not need a separate Stack Deck account. The developer does not operate a separate game-account server. Android or browser controls can remove local data; doing so loses progress on that device.

On Yandex Games, progress and settings may be saved to your Yandex player profile when that service is available. A leaderboard receives your score and information associated with the entry; an available profile name and avatar are used to display it. Clearing local data does not erase cloud saves or leaderboard entries. Use Yandex account controls to manage platform data, or contact the developer with a question.

The connected VK version may receive your public profile name, avatar and identifier to display the player. This version has no separate Stack Deck sign-in, VK cloud saves or VK online leaderboard. The Android version stores game progress locally.

## Advertising

Browser versions use the platform's advertising service. The Android version uses Yandex Mobile Ads. Loading an advertisement connects the app to the advertising service, which receives network information, including an IP address, and information contained in the ad request.

The Android version sends restricted-processing settings and does not send consent for personalized advertising. Location transmission and additional app advertising analytics are disabled. The Android package excludes Google's advertising-ID permission. These settings do not mean that advertising works without network communication or that the platform's own policies no longer apply.

Read the [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/). The VK version is also subject to [VK's Privacy Policy](https://vk.com/privacy). Distribution through RuStore is subject to the [RuStore Privacy Policy](https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/privacy-policy-users); this does not mean that game saves are sent to RuStore.

## Support and your choices

The game does not request your phone number, address, contacts, photos, microphone access or payment details. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message to answer you. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal details. Support correspondence is retained for handling the request and applicable obligations; you can request deletion at the same address.

For access, correction or deletion requests, describe the issue and the platform you use without sending an account password. Data held by Yandex, VK, RuStore or an email provider is also governed by that service's policies. The developer does not promise to erase records from those services independently.

Android advertising restrictions apply to all players, including children and players of unknown age. The game does not invite children to enter personal information. A parent or guardian may contact the address above. Playing the game does not itself provide consent to personalized advertising.

If data practices change, this page will be updated with a new date. Publishing an update does not by itself authorize a new use that requires separate consent.
